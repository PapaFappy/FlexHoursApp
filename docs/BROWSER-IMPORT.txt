FlexHours browser edition — no API key or OAuth setup

1. Extract the ZIP and run bin/FlexHours-Browser.exe.
2. Select the month to calculate. Leave As of set to today for today's balance.
3. Click Import from BambooHR.
4. Enter your company subdomain (e.g. mycompany) or BambooHR website address.
5. Click Open BambooHR. It opens a separate Edge/Chrome window.
6. Sign in normally, including any company SSO or MFA. Open your own Timesheet
   for the selected month and keep only one BambooHR tab open in that window.
7. Return to FlexHours and click Read summary.
8. Check the figures and confirm the page is for the selected month AND year.
   BambooHR's summary may show the month without the year.
9. If you know the holiday/leave hours dated AFTER the As of date that are
   already included in Pay Period total, enter them and tick the confirmation.
   Enter 0 if there are no future credits. Include BOTH holidays and approved
   paid leave, without counting either twice.
10. Click Import into month.

Only the visible summary is imported. It cannot tell us which dates time off
belongs to. Without confirmed future credits, monthly remaining hours work,
but Ahead/Behind shows Check leave. This is deliberate: unverified future
leave must not be presented as hours already earned through today.

Example from your October summary:
 - Pay Period total: 133h 34m
 - REG: 128h 34m
 - Time Off: 5h
 - October 2026 target: 176h; remaining: 42h 26m.
 - If all 5h are future credits, balance on 22 Oct is +34m.
 - If all 5h are on/before 22 Oct, balance is +5h 34m.

Import again whenever you want an updated total. This version does not sync on
a schedule or automatically click through your account. If you change As of
after importing, reimport and reconfirm future credits for that date.

Fallback: if browser automation is blocked, select and copy the visible
timesheet summary in your normal browser, then use Paste summary. This uses
the same parser and preview. No cookie export or browser extension is needed.

Your session stays in an app-specific Edge/Chrome profile on your computer:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\FlexHours\BambooBrowser
It is separate from your normal browser profile. Sign in again if BambooHR
expires it. The app never reads or exports passwords, cookies or tokens.
It reads visible text only, on your entered company's HTTPS BambooHR domain.
The separate browser uses a random local debugging port to allow this read;
close its window when finished. Company browser policies may disable this.

Imported totals save to the same months.json used by earlier app versions.
Use manual entries returns the month to editable total/leave fields. Previous
manual leave entries remain available. Do not run two app versions together.

Supported summary format is the English month summary shown in your screenshot:
133h 34m / Pay Period / Oct 1-31 / Paid Hours / 128h 34m REG / 5h Time Off.
Unsupported or conflicting figures are rejected. If BambooHR changes its page
layout or language, use Paste summary or manual entry until the parser is updated.

Validation: parser, calculation and persistence tests pass. Desktop import
window renders correctly. Live BambooHR extraction was not exercised in this audit against
your signed-in page. Local loopback networking is restricted in the build
environment, so the actual browser-to-app connection could not be tested here.

The earlier OAuth service is not included in this browser-only edition.

Saved-state update: the selected month, comparison date, Today preference,
monthly inputs and last import are restored on launch. Today advances daily;
BambooHR totals remain a saved snapshot until you import again.


Audit update:
The total must include worked hours only through the comparison date. A monthly
summary cannot reconstruct an earlier daily work ledger. New imports confirm
this condition. Future credits cannot exceed the total or remaining weekdays.
Pending/failed reads clear the old summary and block import until finished.
Closing the dialog cancels its browser request; editing credits clears their
confirmation. No network login or cookie-reuse test was performed in this audit.
