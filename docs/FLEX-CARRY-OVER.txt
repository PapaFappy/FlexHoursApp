Monthly carry-over and quarterly reset

Monthly difference = credited Pay Period hours minus the full monthly target.
Pay Period hours include paid holidays and approved paid leave, as in BambooHR.
Required monthly hours remain Monday-Friday days x 8h.

Carry-over = previous completed month's FINAL flex balance, capped at +4h/-4h.
Final flex balance = monthly difference + carry-over. The final displayed
balance is never capped. Calculations use minutes to preserve partial hours.

January, April, July and October always start with 0 carry-over. A new year's
January starts at 0, regardless of the preceding December balance.

The main window has three flex cards and a Flex balances by month history tab.
Double-click a history row to open that month. Green rows start a new quarter.
The original Ahead/Behind card is the balance through the selected date BEFORE
carry-over. The new monthly difference compares against the FULL-month target.
An ongoing month's flex balance is provisional, not an end-of-month result.
Remaining hours in the top cards are still the contractual monthly shortfall
before carry-over; the flex cards show the separate adjustment.

To record a completed month:
 - Select the past month, enter or import its final Pay Period total.
 - Tick Month total confirmed complete once the full total is correct.
 - Its final flex balance can now carry to the following month (within the
   same quarter). You can confirm on the last calendar date of the month too.
 - Updating a completed month's total recalculates dependent months instantly.

If a needed previous month is missing or not marked complete, the carry/final
balance shows Pending with a reason. The app does not silently assume 0.
Enter the missing month's final total and mark it complete to resolve this.
At a quarter reset, no prior-month data is needed.
Opening an empty month does not count it as a recorded zero-hours month.
Confirming a completed month with a zero total explicitly records zero hours.

All source data, completion flags and calculated balances save automatically.
Balances are recalculated from source data when opening the app and saving.
Existing saved months are preserved. Earlier versions did not record completion;
confirm their past-month final totals before carrying them forward.

Example:
October:  +6h difference,  0h carry, +6h final.
November: -7h difference, +4h carry, -3h final.
December: +5h difference, -3h carry, +2h final.
January:  -2h difference,  0h carry, -2h final (reset).
